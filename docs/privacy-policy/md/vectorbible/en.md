# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 17, 2026_

This Privacy Policy describes how Vector Bible: Verse Search ("the App") handles your information.

## 1. Information We Collect

The App **does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal or usage data**. It has no
account or sign-in, and contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services.

## 2. Data Stored on Your Device

The following stays on your device only and is never sent to us or to anyone else:

- **Your searches** – processed entirely on the device by the built-in search model.
- **Recent searches and bookmarked verses** – saved in the App's private storage so you can find them again.

You can clear recent searches in the App and remove bookmarks at any time. Uninstalling the App deletes
all of this data.

## 3. Network Use

Searching works offline. The App only connects to the internet if you choose to download an optional,
larger search model from the Models page. That download comes from a file server; like any web
request, it reveals your IP address to that server, but no personal data, searches or bookmarks are sent.

## 4. Sharing

When you tap Share or Copy, the verse text is passed to the app you choose (or to the clipboard). This
happens only on your request, and what that app does with it is governed by its own privacy policy.

## 5. Children's Privacy

The App does not collect personal data from anyone, including children under 13.

## 6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new
"Last updated" date.
